    About the data

    ADA parking is held in the short-term and long-term lots, even when a lot shows full, and isn't included in the counts above. Cell-phone lots for pickups, bike racks near the terminal entrances. No RVs on airport property.

    Parking data comes from the airport's own feed at flysba.santabarbaraca.gov, which refreshes about every three minutes. Times are Pacific.

    Flight data is tracking only, no predictions yet. Source: Infax's FIDS widget, embedded on the airport's own arrivals & departures page. Live board, not a schedule archive — this reads back what's already been polled and stored.
